    Inclusion is our identity

    Designed with, not just for.

    We believe inclusion goes beyond featuring persons with disabilities in our marketing. It means listening, inviting people into the design process, and making things that fit real, everyday lives.

    Our vision is to create inclusive, functional and tech-integrated products for persons with disabilities (PWDs), the elderly and the caregivers who support them, without giving up colour, style or joy.

    How we work

    1. 01

      We listen first

      Conversations with PWDs, elders and caregivers about their routines, frustrations and the small things that make getting ready harder than it needs to be.

    2. 02

      We design together

      Community members test samples, tell us what works, and shape every version. We pay contributors for their time and expertise.

    3. 03

      We build for real days

      Function comes first: easy grips, clear labels, gentle materials, and tech that helps rather than complicates.

    Who we design with

    Persons with disabilities

    Including people with limited hand dexterity, low vision, blindness, sensory sensitivities and chronic pain.

    Elders

    Pieces that are comfortable, easy to put on, and easy to tell apart, even with arthritis or changing eyesight.

    Caregivers

    Products that are quick and gentle to use when you're helping someone else, and easy to wash and keep track of.

    What we're working on

    These are early ideas we want to develop with the community. Nothing here is final, and your input decides what we build.

    • Concept

      Easy-grip scrunchies

      Wider bands and pull tabs so they can be put on and taken off with one hand or limited grip strength.

    • Concept

      Adaptive bonnets

      Bonnets with magnetic or wide-tab closures instead of tight elastic, gentle on sensitive scalps and easy for caregivers.

    • Concept

      Tactile & braille size tags

      Raised-dot and braille labels so sizes and colours can be identified by touch.

    • Concept

      Scan-to-hear tags

      QR/NFC tags that play an audio description, care instructions and colour name on any phone with a screen reader.

    • Concept

      High-contrast packaging

      Large-print, high-contrast boxes that are easy to open, with no fiddly plastic.

    • Concept

      Accessible ordering

      Order by voice note, text or form, whatever is easiest for you. We'll read everything back to confirm.

    Accessibility statement

    This site is built for everyone

    We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Here's what's built in:

    • Works with screen readers (TalkBack, VoiceOver, NVDA, JAWS), with clear headings, labelled links and landmarks.
    • Fully usable with a keyboard or switch. Press Tab to move, and look for the bright yellow focus outline.
    • Adjustable text size, from 90% up to 200%.
    • High-contrast mode, plus colour contrast that meets AA by default.
    • Readable-font mode with extra letter and word spacing for dyslexia and low vision.
    • Option to stop all motion and animation. We also respect your device's "reduce motion" setting.
    • Text descriptions on all images and illustrations.
    • Simple navigation with a "Skip to main content" link at the top.

    Open the yellow accessibility button in the bottom-right corner to change these settings. Your choices are remembered on this device.
